# PRODUCT.md — A Little Peek into Haisu’s World (Haisu 的小世界)

## Purpose & Context
An intimate, warm, slightly playful digital family album created for private elementary school parent interviews. Its goal is to let admissions teachers get to know the real Haisu in 1–2 minutes when conversation naturally invites a visual peek into his everyday world.

## Core Principles (from Editorial Brief)
1. **Concrete Actions Over Trait Checklists**: Tell stories through real, specific actions, humor, and honest imperfections — never package Haisu into a "list of impressive qualities," capability ratings, achievement walls, or admissions brochure copy.
2. **Strict Fact & Media Integrity**:
   - Never fabricate experiences, quotes, psychological interpretations, or personality evaluations.
   - Never generate AI substitute photos of children.
   - Do not misrepresent *Journey to the West* as independent solo reading until the exact reading mode (shared reading, audio-assisted, etc.) is confirmed.
   - Missing media slots are gracefully handled with text narrative or clearly marked as `待补充` (To be added) in Parent Prep mode.
   - No empty "Watch Intro Video" button unless an intro video file is actually present.
3. **Interview-First Ergonomics**:
   - Designed as an optional companion window during a parent conversation, not a formal presentation meant to be played start-to-finish.
   - Instant jump controls allow parents to open a single specific story (`01`, `02`, or `03`) in one tap.
   - Videos are click-to-play with poster frames, native controls, no autoplaying audio, and optional English subtitles (`.vtt`) for Chinese original audio.
4. **Privacy & Local-First Safety**:
   - Stored in `/usr/local/google/home/lishuang/haisu` (`chmod 700`) outside of world-readable CitC workspaces.
   - No automatic public deployment or weak frontend-only password gates.
